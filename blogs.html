Janad University Celebrates the Fifth Graduating Class "Makers of the Future"

Janad University launched celebrations for its fifth graduating class, "Makers of the Future," under the patronage of University President Dr. Mohiuddin Al-Qubati. The ceremonies, held over a full week, honored graduates from all three colleges — Medical Sciences, Engineering & IT, and Administrative Sciences & Humanities [citation:5].

In his address, President Al-Qubati emphasized that the name "Makers of the Future" carries a profound message: the future is not awaited but built through knowledge, work, and creativity. He praised the graduates for proving that ambition creates achievement and determination leads to success [citation:5].

The celebrations included honoring top students from each department, distributing certificates, and featuring artistic and national performances. The university continues to strengthen its position as a leading private institution in Taiz, committed to preparing graduates who are ready for the job market and community service.
