Who We Are

A University of Three Distinguished Colleges

Al Janad University for Science and Technology is a private higher education institution established in the academic year 2018–2019. It was officially inaugurated on October 2, 2018, under the Ministry of Higher Education license No. (11) of 2018 [citation:2].

The university consists of three colleges offering a total of 21 academic programs leading to bachelor's degrees. Programs were selected based on local and regional market needs and built academically to meet accreditation standards [citation:2].

  • College of Medical Sciences
  • College of Engineering and Information Technology
  • College of Administrative Sciences and Humanities
