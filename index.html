A private higher education institution established in 2018–2019 in Taiz, Yemen. Officially inaugurated on October 2, 2018, under the Ministry of Higher Education license No. (11) of 2018.

Discover Janad
Our Identity

Vision, Mission & Goals

Al Janad University for Science and Technology strives for excellence in teaching, learning, scientific research, and community service.

Vision

To locally lead and regionally compete in education and scientific research, effectively contribute to community service, foster national allegiance, and contribute to sustainable development.

Mission

To strive for excellence in teaching, learning, scientific research and community service through a motivating educational environment that keeps up with quality standards.

Goals

Excellence in teaching and learning, excellence in scientific research, enhancing effective partnership in the community, developing financial resources, and developing human resources capabilities.

Academics

University Colleges

Three distinguished colleges offering 21 academic programs designed to meet local and regional market needs, built to academic accreditation standards.

Administrative Sciences & Humanities

Programs in business administration, accounting, management information systems, English language and translation, Sharia and law, and more.

Engineering & Information Technology

Civil engineering, architecture, mechatronics, interior design, IT, networks and cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and data science.

Medical Sciences

Dentistry, pharmacy, Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm D), medical laboratories, therapeutic nutrition, and nursing.
